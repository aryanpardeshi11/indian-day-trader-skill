1. OVERALL ARCHITECTURE & WORKFLOW MODES
====================================================================================================
The Indian Day Trader skill provides modular, deterministic command-line engines covering four 
primary trading segments:
  • EQ_DELIVERY   : Equity Delivery / Cash Swing Trading (0% STT Buy, 0.1% STT Sell, DP charges)
  • EQ_INTRADAY   : Equity Day Trading (0.025% STT Sell, 5x leverage max, mandatory 15:15 IST square-off)
  • FNO_FUT       : NSE Futures (0.02% STT Sell, span+exposure margin, contract expiry roll)
  • FNO_OPT       : NSE Options (0.1% STT on Premium Sell / 0.125% Exercise, Black-Scholes Greeks, 
                    decay & volatility models)

Every command can be executed in three operational modes:
  A. Full Unified Pipeline (run_all.py): Runs news sentiment, TV cloud indicators, market structure,
     Monte Carlo backtests, 2026 tax gating, and stages paper orders in a single end-to-end run.
  B. Guided Interactive Terminal Menu (menu.py): Interactive numeric prompts for quick analysis.
  C. Granular Standalone CLI Execution: Invoking specific standalone engines for specialized tasks
     such as Option Chain Greeks, Sector Rotation, or Custom Trade Sizing.
